# Worked Example: Three Fictional Cases

An entirely fictional Riverside Photography Workshop, sending the same fixed follow-up template to three different situations.

## Case A: Single Recipient

Maya attended alone. During the session, she specifically asked about getting sharp shots in low light without a tripod. A low-light cheat sheet resource exists and has a working link.

## Case B: Two Recipients Together

Tom and Lisa attended together as a couple. Tom asked about landscape composition; Lisa asked about editing software recommendations. Both are on the same email.

## Case C: Recipient Forwarding to Someone Not There

Priya attended representing her camera club. She needs to forward the workshop material to Dev, the club's events coordinator, who was not at the workshop and will not be on this email directly. Priya herself asked about portrait lighting setups.
