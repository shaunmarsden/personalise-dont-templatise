# Worked Example

A fictional photography workshop's follow-up template, sent to three different situations without ever changing its structure.

- [the-fixed-template.md](the-fixed-template.md): the template itself, never restructured
- [inputs.md](inputs.md): three cases, a single recipient, two recipients together, and one recipient forwarding to someone who was not there
- [output.md](output.md): the three drafted messages
- [review.md](review.md): whether the template stayed genuinely fixed, whether both recipients in the two-person case were kept in the second person, and whether the forward-to-someone-else block was correctly separated from the recipient's own message
